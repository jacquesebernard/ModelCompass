# Data rights and integration status

The code's MIT license grants no rights to third-party data or services.

Reviewed 2026-10-27: [Data Platform Terms v1.1](https://artificialanalysiscdn.com/legal/ProDataPlatformTerms.pdf), sections 2, 5 and 11. Operators must verify their agreement: raw-data redistribution and third-party dashboards are restricted; a model-selection product offered to third parties requires prior written consent. Shared charts require the supplied AA logo; displayed metrics require linked attribution. The app includes a source link, but no substitute logo. External branding permissions remain unresolved. The terms reviewed do not establish a cache TTL; confirm retention permission and deletion duties with AA. Live access is local and opt-in. No authentic dataset or live screenshot is included.

The [MCP docs](https://artificialanalysis.ai/data-api/docs/mcp) establish unattended API-key authentication. [API reference](https://artificialanalysis.ai/data-api/docs/reference) field paths inform normalization, but tool names/arguments are obtained from authenticated MCP discovery. Authenticated schema discovery and Free-tier live retrieval were verified locally on 2026-10-27. Other subscription entitlements have not been exercised. The app never substitutes scraping, REST calls or another data vendor.

Synthetic fixtures are independently authored fictional examples and are MIT licensed with this code. Keep caches, real response payloads and live screenshots out of public contributions. Before sending live candidate metrics to a chat provider, verify that your agreement permits that processing. Publishing the code does not authorize publishing the connected data.
